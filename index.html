Quick Guide

Set the cable once, import sounds, bind keys, and route what you hear separately from what Discord receives.

1. Discord Routing

Set freqx output to CABLE Input. In Discord, set input to CABLE Output and output to headphones or speakers.

2. Add Sounds

Use Import Track to copy audio into the local library. Click a sound card to play it into the Discord mix.

3. Keybinds

Bind a key on any sound card for quick triggers. Bind Stop Key to stop every playing sound immediately.

4. Mixer

Microphone controls your voice level, Soundboard controls meme volume, and Main Output controls the final Discord feed.

5. Hear Locally

Use Hear Meme Sounds On to choose where your local preview plays. Pick headphones to avoid speaker bleed.

6. Safety Checks

Refresh devices after installing VB-CABLE. Use Tone to test routing before joining a voice channel.
